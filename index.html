NATURAL STONE SELECTION

Premium South African & Imported Granite

Granite is one of nature’s hardest minerals. Our stone selections resist weathering, acid rain, and sun exposure to retain their mirror polish for decades.

Rustenburg Black

South Africa’s premier dark granite. Fine-grained with high density, perfect for gold leaf lettering.

Belfast Black

Deep jet black with uniform texture. Unmatched contrast for laser photo engraving and portraits.

African Red

Rich terracotta and ruby mineral patterns. A distinguished, regal choice for executive family ledgers.

Platinum Pearl

Silver and slate crystalline texture. Highly reflective, low-maintenance, and resilient to all weather.
