COMMON QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

Do you handle cemetery permits?

Yes. Every cemetery has specific height, width, and concrete foundation regulations. We submit the paperwork and obtain the required municipal approvals on your behalf.

Can I add a ceramic photo or custom artwork?

Absolutely. We provide kiln-fired ceramic photo plaques and precision diamond/laser engravings that withstand outdoor sun and weather without fading.

How long does manufacturing and installation take?

Standard brochure designs are typically crafted and installed within 2 to 4 weeks once the digital inscription proof is signed off and permit granted.

Do you deliver outside Gauteng?

Yes. Everstone provides safe transport and professional cemetery installation across all nine South African provinces.
