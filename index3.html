TAILORED SERVICE · SOUTH AFRICAN HERITAGE

Dignified Guidance for Every Family

We understand the profound cultural significance of memorials and unveiling ceremonies. Our experienced team manages every detail from municipal cemetery permits to clan inscriptions and rural delivery.

01

Unveiling Ceremony Commitments

We work closely with your family timeline for ukwembula itshe ceremonies, ensuring guaranteed on-site erection ahead of your Easter, Winter, or December gatherings.

  • Guaranteed completion dates in writing
  • Pre-ceremony stone polishing & site clearance
02

Authentic Solid Granite

Every tombstone is carved from genuine South African natural stone—including Rustenburg Black (Impala), Belfast Jet Black, and African Red—engineered to endure high UV and heavy summer storms.

  • High-density stone that never fades or stains
  • Reinforced concrete sub-plinth foundations
03

Permits & Multilingual Inscriptions

We handle municipal permit filings, cemetery fees, and berm compliance. We also expertly engrave clan names (isithakazelo) in isiZulu, isiXhosa, Sesotho, Afrikaans, and English.

  • Free digital layout proof before carving
  • Delivery to municipal cemeteries & rural homes
